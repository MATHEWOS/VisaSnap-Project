Privacy Policy

VisaSnap processes selected photos in your browser on your device. The core photo tool does not upload your image to a server.

The app stores only normal browser cache files needed for offline use, including app code, icons, and layout assets. Clearing browser site data removes this cache.

If ads, paid upgrades, or sponsored links are enabled, payments and advertising may be handled by third-party providers such as Stripe, Google, or affiliate partners. Those providers process data under their own policies.

VisaSnap links to official government visa websites. Those external sites have their own privacy practices.

Last updated: June 18, 2026.
