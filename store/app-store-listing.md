# App Store Listing Draft

## Name

VisaSnap

## Subtitle

Passport and e-visa photos

## Promotional Text

Create passport-style and e-visa photos on your phone with simple local cropping, common visa sizes, and offline access after first launch.

## Description

VisaSnap helps travelers prepare passport-style and e-visa photos from their phone.

Take a new photo or choose one from your library, position it inside common visa photo sizes, choose a plain background color, and download a JPG for use with official visa portals.

The core photo tool runs locally on your device. VisaSnap does not upload your selected photo to a server, and it does not brighten, sharpen, filter, mirror, flip, or replace your image.

VisaSnap also includes quick links to official visa and immigration websites for popular destinations. Always confirm the current photo rules, fees, and eligibility on the destination government's official site before submitting.
